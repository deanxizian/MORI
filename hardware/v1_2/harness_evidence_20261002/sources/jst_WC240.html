WC-240 Hand Crimping Tool

The WC-240 is an ergonomically designed, parallel action handtool, designed specifically to crimp our loose piece SPH-002T-P0.5S contact.

Specifications:

  • Fitted with a flap locator to ensure the correct positioning of the contact during crimping
  • Ratchet mechanism for crimp conformation
  • Ergonomically designed soft handles
  • Precision ground dies
  • Safe operation
  • 3 cavities to suit wire sizes 24awg, 26awg and 28/30awg
