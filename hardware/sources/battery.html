Battery
  • 7.4V 2.0Ah polymer Li-Ion battery pack is made of two  Polymer Li-ion 3.7V 2000 mAh (PL-605060-2C)   with prewired   PCB (8.0A) & 4.2 Amp polyswitch
  • Wrapped by white PVC heat shrink tube
Voltage Voltage: 7.4 V (working)   8.4 V ( peak)  5.5 V ( cut-off)
Capacity 2000 mAh or 14.8 wh
Protection
  • One PCB (8.0A)installed with the battery pack and protects the battery from
    • Overcharge       (>8.4V)
    • Over discharge  ( <5.5V)
    • Over drain ( > 8.0 Amp)
    • Short circuits
  • One 4.2 Amp polyswitch  installed to limit  max. discharging current at 4.2A and to protect wrong polarity
Prewired 6" 22 AWG open end wire
Charging rate 1.2A (recommended) 
Max. Discharging Rate 4.2 Amp limited by polyswitch ( lower rate available upon request )
Dimensions (LxWxH) 66mm(2.6") x 54mm(2.1") x 20mm(0.8")  
  • Similar size as the pack (18650 2S Side by Side configuration) below.
  • Li-Ion 18650 7.4 V 2000mah battery module with PCB (protection IC) (2LI18650RPB01)
  • Li-Ion 18650 7.4V 2200mah battery module with PCB (protection IC) (LC-18650S2WR-2200)  
  • Li-Ion 18650 7.4V 2600mah battery module with PCB (protection IC) (LC-18650S2WR-2600)  
Weight 3.4Oz (95grams)
Smart Tips
  • You may choose our Smart 7.4V Li-Ion Battery Charger (1.2A) to recharge this battery pack.  The estimated charging time is 2.5 hours
  • If you connect two of these modules in parallel, you can build a 7.4V 4.0 Ah battery pack with a 8.4 Amp Max. discharge rate.
  • Must read our battery knowledge: Why Li-ion battery pack has 0 voltage?  
Applications
  • External battery pack to run 6V DC devices with 4.2A Max current drawn. Such as digital camera or camcorder
  • 7.4VDC device with 4.2A Max current drawn
Warning
  • Li-Ion Battery may explode if charging or discharging improperly. User must have knowledge on how to charge and discharge Li-Ion battery before making Li-Ion Battery Pack.
