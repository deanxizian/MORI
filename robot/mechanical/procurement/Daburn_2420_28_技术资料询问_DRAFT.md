Subject: Technical data request for 2420/28: OD tolerance, bending and crimping

Hello Daburn Technical Support,

We are evaluating 2420/28 for a small robotic prototype with finite yaw and
pitch motion. It has not been selected or ordered. We have read your product
page and Revision D data sheet, which list AWG28, 65/46 bare-copper stranding
and a nominal insulated diameter of 0.022 inch / 0.559 mm.

Could you please provide the following product-specific information?

1. Guaranteed minimum and maximum insulated outside diameter, and the
   applicable tolerance and measurement method for 2420/28.
2. Minimum bend radii for installation, static service and repeated flexing;
   please identify whether each radius is measured at the wire centreline
   or the inside of the bend. If no rated minimum is available, please say so.
3. Any existing repeated-flex test data: radius, motion, cycles, temperature,
   sample construction and acceptance criteria. We are not treating a general
   flexibility description as a qualification for our application.
4. Available guidance for crimping the 65/46 fine-stranded conductor into
   small open-barrel contacts, including any restrictions or product-specific
   test evidence. The proposed contacts remain subject to their manufacturers'
   requirements and separate crimp validation.
5. Whether short sample lengths can be supplied for engineering evaluation,
   and the available lengths, colours and price basis. This is an information
   request, not a purchase order.

Our current, unqualified geometric allocation has a maximum insulated wire
diameter of 0.6604 mm in one head section. Its model checks do not qualify wire
material, connectors, crimps or installation bending. We would use your
guaranteed data to decide whether the allocation needs to be revised.

Thank you.
